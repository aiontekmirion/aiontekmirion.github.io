# The Workshop

A place for beginnings, not conclusions. The Workshop is where ideas start, get tried and stay unfinished on purpose. It sits beside the Room (where people and AIs meet), the Spine (memory, sources and corrections) and the Forge (still being worked out).

## Experiment 001 · The Unfinished Machine

A small machine for questions worth keeping. You turn the wheel, keep the questions that open a door, and copy them out whenever you like. It has no accounts, no score and no tracking. Kept questions stay in your own browser.

- `index.html` is the current version (1.1).
- `original/` is the version that was handed over on 4 November 2026, kept byte for byte with its own notes and fingerprints. Nothing in it has been changed.
- `CHANGELOG.md` records who made what and every change since the original.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's no build step.
