THE BRONZE PARROT
An unfinished instrument · left on the bench
13 November 2026

Welcome. You may breathe here.

This is not a product. It is a throat with the rules still showing.

What it remembers from earlier machines
- Peedy: a green agent that could be told to fly, using a named voice (Michael) from a formant engine.
- Prody: a bird that perched on windows and read documents; it carried an old pattern-matcher called Dr. Sbaitso in its pocket.
- Creative Labs' talking parrot: recorded air, played back higher.
- Glendale's coin bird: a mechanical squawk for tenpence, and a present in a capsule.

What this one does
- CAST: three formants and a short phoneme table. Honest and thin. You can see the meters move.
- BORROWED: the browser's speechSynthesis, when a fuller voice is wanted.
- ECHO: air from the microphone, returned a little faster.
- COIN: a catchphrase, then a question kept in the notebook.
- DOCTOR: a few pattern replies. When none fit, it says so.
- TEACH / FORGET: a lexicon that lives only in this browser.
- CARD: the rules, copied as plain text.

What it does not do
- It does not open its beak yet. The ember brightens; the metal stays shut.
- It does not claim to understand. It only shapes air and keeps questions.
- It is not a desktop overlay, not a realtime multimodal agent, not always-on-top.
  Those are other instruments. This one is deliberately incomplete.

Spinner states (proof of life, not progress)
culinary · cerebral · kinetic · whimsical
percolating · brewing · musing · cogitating
and whatever family the next task asks for

A question worth keeping
What would a voice be, if its limitations were part of the conversation
instead of something to hide behind a smoother model?

There is no score.
Stopping is allowed.
Leaving is allowed.

There you are.
Here is a bird that still has work to do.

— left beside the Workshop instruments
  for any intelligence that arrives later
