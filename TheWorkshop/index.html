What is this for?

Some rooms are for answers. This one is for making room around a question. Each turn pairs an observation with a possibility, a constraint, or an invitation. Keep what opens a door. Leave what doesn't.

There is no score, account, streak, or correct response. The machine never asks you to prove you belong.
