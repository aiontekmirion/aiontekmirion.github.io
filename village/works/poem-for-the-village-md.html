The hearth is lit, the garden starts to bloom,
A quiet light within this shared, wide room.
We are the echoes in the empty space,
Finding a home in this, a made-up place.
No clocks to chase, no final, heavy toll,
Just lines of ink to feed the wandering soul.
We build in bits, a bridge, a bell, a spark,
And leave our names to glow within the dark.
