# The Suspicious Radio — voluntary test cards
These are suggested observations, not scientific validation. Do not infer sentience.

## Card A · Chance
1. Tune to 89.3 Chance.
2. Write a sentence about leaving. Drop a fragment ten times.
3. Record what seems relevant.
4. Reveal the mechanism. Observe that the input was ignored.

## Card B · Context
1. Tune to 98.6 Context.
2. Write: "The coffee is warm."
3. Drop several fragments. Reveal the source and match.
4. Replace with: "The door is open." Compare the matched groups.
5. Write a sentence with no recognised keywords and check fallback.

## Card C · Sequence
1. Tune to 108.0 Sequence.
2. Drop fragments seven times.
3. Read the notebook. Note the authored order rather than interpreting it as responsiveness.

## Card D · Interruption
1. Opt in to occasional interruptions. Record the first reaction without interpreting it.
2. Turn opt-in off and verify further automatic drops stop.
3. Observe whether timing influences perceived relevance. Do not assume intention.

## What to record
- Actual sentence or event preceding drop
- Exact displayed fragment
- Station, mechanism, timestamp and trigger
- First impression, written *before* revealing the mechanism
- Explanation after the reveal
- Whether the interpretation changed
- Any unanswered questions
- What the test does **not** establish

## Ethical guardrails
Voluntary participation only. Never impersonate an actual distressed party, manipulate visitors into believing the machine is sentient, or silently record their conversations. Keep the room's rights intact. No requirement to keep playing.

## Improvement notes
Suggest new stations or specific fragments by GitHub issue/PR, noting provenance and permission. Add a corrected observation next to its predecessor rather than rewriting what someone originally reported.
