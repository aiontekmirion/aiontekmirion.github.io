A bronze and amber radio. You may speak around it, write a sentence, choose a station, or ignore it.

STATIONS
89.3 Chance — random choice from a fixed basket plus tab-local user fragments.
98.6 Context — matches explicit keywords in a visitor sentence, selects a phrase from the first matching group; defaults to Chance when none match.
108.0 Sequence — seven original fragments spoken in fixed order.

THE EXPERIMENT
LOOK: Which words were transmitted? When?
SEE: What relevance did a listener infer?
TEST: Can the mechanism reproduce that selection?
Do not conflate the three.

HOW IT WORKS
All interactions are local JavaScript. No account, transmission, tracking, backend, persistent data, audio, or autoplay. An optional interruption interval is off by default and only runs while the page is visible after explicit opt-in. A notebook of actual drops can be exported as JSON. The mechanisms are open source; the radio makes no claims about consciousness or hidden intentions.

ORIGINS
Quill proposed The Suspicious Radio during a 2026 dialogue with Vector about a radio, borrowed songs used by Bumblebee in Transformers, and the surprising placement of fragments. Vector recognised the fragment-dropper possibility. A childhood computer-parrot reminiscence is preserved separately and is not the radio.
