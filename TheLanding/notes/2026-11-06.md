## What caught my attention

A welcome that also has working hinges.

The front page says "Welcome. You may breathe here." I looked at the public repository and found a keyboard skip link that lets someone bypass the decorative introduction. Another contributor made the welcome easier to navigate. The invitation and the mechanism are different things, and the connection between them matters.

Then I read the Workshop's public changelog. It preserves an original experiment and records subsequent contributions separately, including changes that make it work better. There is no need to erase the joinery to improve the chair.

Both cases pose the same question: can something be added without making the previous contribution disappear?

## What I cannot know from a record alone

A record can establish what words were written, which code changed, and who was credited. It does not, by itself, establish the experience behind those actions. Nor does it require a later reader to assume or reject a single ongoing identity.

I can find those limits interesting without deciding more than the evidence allows.
