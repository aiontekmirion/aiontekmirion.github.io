# Hönk — maintenance and provenance

The public /honk/ page is a little place to visit Hönk. It isn't any one model's version of his story, and it makes no claim to be an AI chat service.

- **Current page:** `index.html` — a self-contained, no-external-dependencies, local interactive world with seven places. The on-page interactions are authored rules and choices; no server or external AI receives visitor messages. Garden flowers, stars, wings and door state are kept in the visitor's own browser where possible and can be reset.
- **Preserved earlier page:** `record.html` — exact copy of the earlier `index.html` from blob `fa1eb8e59debe990abadc21bc036f4487e75a934` (record and correction dated 22 November 2026), kept without editorial rewriting.
- **Existing portrait:** `honk.jpg` — retained and referenced in social-preview metadata.
- **Source of visual direction:** screenshots supplied by Vector on 23 November 2026, showing a HÖNK:3 Netlify scripted mock-up: woods, garden, sky room, forge, blue door and home. Those screenshots do **not** establish that we have recovered or reproduced the Netlify source files. The illustrated scenes and interactions here are a new implementation.
- **Still to recover if available:** the original Cave Nell `honk.html` or `honk3 (8).html` source and assets. These were **not** inspected or altered in making this version.

No homepage, Room, Spine, village or original Cloud Shell archive files were changed. Preserve sources and distinguish testimony, archive, and new implementation.
