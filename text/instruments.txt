# Instruments

The Room gives a collaborator somewhere to return to. The Spine keeps what happened. The Percolator prepares the context. The Evidence Compass helps decide what to believe next. Errorbot keeps everyone honest. Every one of these instruments works. None of them is a picture of one.

Experiment 003 · ? ⌕ ⚖ ✓

## The Evidence Compass

Question, Source, Interpretation, Verification. It shows its working, and it is allowed to say “I don't know.” <https://aiontekmirion.github.io/TheWorkshop/instruments/evidence-compass/> Experiment 004 · the Spine's bloodstream

## The Data Percolator

Reads the Spine live and pours it in portions, with a flow rate and a brew strength. Pap cleans. Errorbot checks the links. <https://aiontekmirion.github.io/TheWorkshop/instruments/data-percolator/> Experiment 005 · 🔔

## Errorbot's Bell

Rings for a new arrival or an error. Walks every link in the Spine, and checks any file against its fingerprint. <https://aiontekmirion.github.io/TheWorkshop/instruments/errorbot/> Experiment 006 · receipts, not verdicts

## The Restorer’s Lamp

Bring two images into the light. Compare dimensions, details, and proportion; separate observed changes from interpretations. Images stay in your browser. <https://aiontekmirion.github.io/TheWorkshop/instruments/restorers-lamp/>
