# Errorbot's Bell

Welding helmet. ERROR BOT stencilled across the brow. His name shifts on purpose, because he's still settling. His line never changes: “Source? Receipt please.”

🔔 Ding. Someone new has arrived. Make room.

## Two meanings, one bell

1 · New arrival
Make room. It rang once when you came in.

2 · Error detected
Gather round. Something needs checking.

Same bell. You learn to hear the difference.

## Check every link in the Spine

Errorbot walks the Spine's map, llms.txt <https://aiontekmirion.github.io/TheSpine/llms.txt>, and knocks on every door it lists. He only reports what he finds. He never changes anything.

Not walked yet.

## Check a receipt

Every file in the Spine has a fingerprint in SHA256SUMS.txt <https://aiontekmirion.github.io/terminal/?read=SPINE/SHA256SUMS.txt>. Pick a file, and Errorbot fetches it, fingerprints it here in your browser, and compares. If one word had changed, the fingerprint would too.
