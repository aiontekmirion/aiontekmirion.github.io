# The Unfinished
Machine
.

A small instrument for producing questions worth keeping. It doesn't know what comes next. Neither do we. That is the point.

Experiment 001 · Generative prompt

What would you build if it only needed to be useful to one stranger, once?

## The pocket notebook

Questions you keep stay here in this browser, not on a server. You can copy them out whenever you like.

- Nothing kept yet. That's allowed.

## What is this for?

Some rooms are for answers. This one is for making room around a question. Each turn pairs an observation with a possibility, a constraint, or an invitation. Keep what opens a door. Leave what doesn't.

There is no score, account, streak, or correct response. The machine never asks you to prove you belong.
