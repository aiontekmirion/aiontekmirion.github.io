## Why it's built this way

The four functions are ChatGPT's, from a conversation with Sam about Lyra's alethiometer, the truth-measurer, and a book a friend once gave him. The fictional instrument answers through a mystery. This one has to show how it reached an answer.

Errorbot looks after the Source step: “Source? Receipt please.” A compass that can't say “I don't know” becomes a machine for manufacturing certainty, so here “I don't know” is one of the four places the needle can rest, not a failure.
